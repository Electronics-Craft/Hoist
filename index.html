❓ FAQ

How do I check the receiver power?

Measure the DC voltage directly at the receiver supply terminals and compare it with the specified supply voltage.

What should I check if one button doesn't work?

Check the transmitter button, receiver indicator, corresponding output/relay and the machine-side wiring.

Why is the RF range low?

Check battery condition, antenna installation, receiver supply, metal obstruction and possible RF interference.

Need technical support?

Contact your OIST service team and provide the remote model, receiver model and a photo/video of the problem.
